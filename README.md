**The Gilded Vault** is a top-down stealth heist on one screen: the thief Wren must steal the glowing Sunset Diamond from a private art gallery at night and get out by the skylight rope. Guards walk patrols with sweeping vision cones and wall cameras pan the rooms. Being seen fills the alarm meter, and at 100% a lockdown gives you 3.5 seconds to break line of sight. Pick your route (the guarded Main Hall, the Side Gallery with its three safes, or the dark, slow Maintenance Corridor) and spend two smoke pellets and one EMP wisely.

Controls: WASD/arrows move · Shift sneak · hold E to interact (loot, safes, cutting cameras) · Q smoke · F EMP · M mute. Screenshot scenes: `?scene=heist|patrol|loot|menu|win|busted`.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
